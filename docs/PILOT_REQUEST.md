**To:** Pengarah Kesihatan Negeri Selangor, Jabatan Kesihatan Negeri Selangor
**Attention:** Unit Survelan, Bahagian Kesihatan Awam
**From:** [names], [university and programme]
**Date:** [date]
**Subject:** Request for aggregate weekly influenza surveillance figures for a student decision-support prototype

Dear Dr [name],

We are [number] undergraduate students at [university]. For a national student competition we built FluSentinel, a prototype that turns weekly influenza figures into an alert level, a plain reason and a proposed action for planners. It is public at [link] and shows every test result, including the ones that failed.

**What we found.** Using only public data (the national sentinel laboratory figures that Malaysia reports to the World Health Organization), a simple alert rule fired in the week the October 2025 surge crossed our alert line when given current figures, and four weeks later when given the figures as published. We could not test anything at state or district level, because no such series is public.

**What we are asking for.** Aggregate weekly figures for Selangor, with no information about any individual, school or facility that could identify a person:

| Item | Level | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Influenza-like illness consultation rate at sentinel clinics | State, and district if available | 2022 to present, weekly |
| Number of influenza clusters reported | District | 2022 to present, weekly |
| Samples tested and number positive for influenza at sentinel sites | State | 2022 to present, weekly |

A table in the format at [link to templates] is enough. If only part of this can be shared, part is still useful. If none can be shared, we would be grateful for ten minutes of advice on what a district health office would actually need from a tool like this.

**How we would use it.** Only to test whether the alert would have fired earlier or more accurately on Selangor's own figures, using the same method and with the rules fixed before the test. We will not publish the figures themselves without your written agreement. We will show you the results before anyone else sees them, whatever they are.

**How we would look after it.** The figures stay with the [number] named team members, on our own computers, and are deleted when you ask or by [date]. We will sign a data-sharing or confidentiality agreement if you have one.

**What you would get.** A short report on how the alert performed on your data, including any failures, and the tool itself if it is of use.

**What this is not.** It is not a clinical tool, not an official reporting system, and not affiliated with the Ministry of Health. Its alert thresholds are our own and would need to be recalculated from your data.

Thank you for considering this.

Yours sincerely,
[names, student emails, supervising lecturer if any]
