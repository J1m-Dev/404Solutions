Who it is for

The user, what is at stake, and where the facilities come from.

Know an influenza surge has started in the week it starts, not four weeks later.

Public influenza figures for Malaysia run about four weeks behind. FluSentinel takes a planner's own newer lab figures and returns an alert level, the kind of wave, a proposed action and the reason for it. A machine-learning classifier adds the chance of a rapid rise in the next four weeks. Every test result is shown, including the ones that failed.

Who this is for, and what is at stake

The user Hypothesis

A planner who must act on influenza but does not see the Ministry of Health's internal figures: for example the infection-control lead of a private hospital group, or a state-government public-health planner.

The decision: when to start school-cluster response and vaccination outreach, and which hospitals to put on standby.

Not yet tested with a person in this role. The tested alert thresholds come from the national sentinel series; a planner's own series would need its own thresholds.

Published costs Published figures

  • Direct medical cost of influenza in Malaysia: MYR 310.9 million over 2016 to 2018, almost all of it hospital admissions.
  • One admission for severe respiratory infection: about MYR 3,900 to 5,400. One clinic visit: about MYR 43 to 60.

Source: "Cross-sectional study of influenza trends and costs in Malaysia between 2016 and 2018", PLOS ONE, 2024. Direct medical costs only. We claim no savings: that needs a user's own admission counts, response costs and what they would do differently.

Facilities on the map

How to verify registered private GP clinics (klinik swasta)

Under Malaysian law (Act 586), private clinics and doctors must be registered with CKAPS, the Private Medical Practice Control Section of the Ministry of Health. Private GP clinics are not plotted here; to check one:

  • CKAPS directory (Ministry of Health): hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac, under the register of Klinik Perubatan Swasta (Am).
  • Malaysian Medical Council: mmc.gov.my, to check that a practitioner is licensed.
  • data.gov.my: the Ministry of Health facilities dataset, filtered by Selangor postcodes.
